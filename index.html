Privacy

Your computer shouldn’t have to send your life to the cloud just to remember it.

DayRecall is built around local-first memory. It records app and browser activity on your machine, keeps the data under your control, and helps you search and revisit your own work without depending on external tracking or cloud storage.

Your Computer
DayRecall
Your Data
Local

Activity data stays on your device and is stored locally with SQLite-backed persistence.

Control

Tracking is user-controlled, with privacy controls and local export & backup workflows.

AI

Optional local AI with Ollama keeps analysis close to the machine when configured.
